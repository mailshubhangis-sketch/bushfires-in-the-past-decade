

# Bushfires — website

Public website for users to see how many major bushfires have been observed in the past decade.

- `index.html` — the whole site (multiple page, tabbed sections: Home, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Australia & NZ, Contribution)
- `assets/` — images used by the page

- The home page will provide information with lit up regions of the major fires around the world with each continent lit up with worst bushfire. When the user hovers over the lit up region, they will get information of the devastation, date and efforts taken to put out the fire.

- The first tab will provide lit up areas where bushfires have been observed in the past 10 years in Asia. When the user hovers over the lit up region, they will get the year of the fire, the devastation caused and the loss of land and animals, life  and property observed.


- The first tab will provide lit up areas where bushfires have been observed in the past 10 years in Europe.When the user hovers over the lit up region, they will get the year of the fire, the devastation caused and the loss of land and animals, life  and property observed.



- The first tab will provide lit up areas where bushfires have been observed in the past 10 years in North America.When the user hovers over the lit up region, they will get the year of the fire, the devastation caused and the loss of land and animals, life  and property observed.



- The first tab will provide lit up areas where bushfires have been observed in the past 10 years in South America.When the user hovers over the lit up region, they will get the year of the fire, the devastation caused and the loss of land and animals, life  and property observed.


- The first tab will provide lit up areas where bushfires have been observed in the past 10 years in Australia and New Zealand.When the user hovers over the lit up region, they will get the year of the fire, the devastation caused and the loss of land and animals, life  and property observed.


- The last tab will ask for contribution from the user to help with support and rehabilitation and assets procurement to put out future fires and also suggest how can we prevent fires.
